3. Internal Google Drive tool

KORDEX uses an internal tool (“KORDEX Drive sync”) to copy its own business documents to and from the company's own Google Drive account, which is shared only with KORDEX's accountant. The tool is used only by KORDEX, accesses only KORDEX's own Google Drive and does not access, collect or share data of any other Google users. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only for this purpose and is not sold, transferred or used for advertising.

4. Who receives the data

  • Hetzner Online GmbH, Germany — hosts our main website server in the EU (processor).
  • Netlify — hosts the StepUp NEO landing page and processes its form submissions and temporary function logs (processor). Processing may take place outside the EEA; see Netlify's privacy notice for transfer information.
  • Porkbun LLC, USA — our e-mail service (processor).
  • Google Ireland Ltd. — Google Drive for our business documents (processor); it does not receive landing-page form submissions through this integration.

Only KORDEX staff handling enquiries have access to form submissions. Form submissions can be forwarded to a KORDEX lead-processing endpoint when one is configured. We will update this notice before enabling any additional external recipient. We do not sell personal data and do not pass your correspondence to manufacturers or other third parties without asking you first.

5. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing and a copy in a usual format, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 15–18, 20 and 21). Write to sourcing@kordex.org; we reply within one month (Art. 12(3)). You can also complain to the supervisory authority: Informacijski pooblaščenec, Dunajska cesta 22, 1000 Ljubljana, gp.ip@ip-rs.si, www.ip-rs.si.

6. Security

The connection is encrypted (HTTPS). Mailbox credentials are kept in a secrets vault. Correspondence is read only by KORDEX staff handling it. If a personal data breach occurs, we notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours where the law requires it (GDPR Art. 33).

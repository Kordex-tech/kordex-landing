3. Who receives the data

Form data is processed by KORDEX staff handling enquiries and by the hosting and form provider Netlify, which keeps temporary function logs. If correspondence continues by e-mail, our e-mail provider also processes the data. The form is currently not connected to a separate KORDEX lead-processing system. Netlify may process data outside the EEA; see Netlify's privacy notice. We do not sell personal data or pass it to manufacturers without your consent.

4. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing and a copy in a usual format, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 15–18, 20 and 21). Write to sourcing@kordex.org; we will reply as soon as possible. You can also complain to the supervisory authority: Informacijski pooblaščenec, Dunajska cesta 22, 1000 Ljubljana, gp.ip@ip-rs.si, www.ip-rs.si.

5. Security

The connection is encrypted (HTTPS). Mailbox credentials are kept in a secrets vault. Correspondence is read only by KORDEX staff handling it. If a personal data breach occurs, we notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours where the law requires it (GDPR Art. 33).
